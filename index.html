Easy Crepes Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix flour and eggs.
  2. Slowly add milk and water, stirring to avoid lumps.
  3. Add melted butter and salt. Mix well.
  4. Heat a pan over medium heat and lightly grease it.
  5. Pour a small amount of batter and swirl to spread.
  6. Cook for about 1–2 minutes, then flip.
  7. Cook the other side until lightly golden.
  8. Serve with your favorite toppings!

Enjoy your homemade crepes!
